HOW WAHALA WORKS

Play through 7 days, from Monday morning to Sunday night. Each day contains 8 possible scenarios, but you only face 6. That means different runs can reveal different stories.

There are multiple endings: career wins, relationship wins, financial survival, total chaos, and secret outcomes.

Your archetype changes your starting resources and unlocks special choices. Your city changes flavour text and some events.

Items, promises, lies, favours and money can come back later. There is no single “correct” route.
